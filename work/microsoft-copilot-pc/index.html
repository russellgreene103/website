Microsoft Copilot+ PC launch

Microsoft Windows & Surface · 2024 · Executive Producer, BUCK

When Microsoft introduced a new class of AI PCs, BUCK helped bring the launch's visual world to life across films, retail, the web and Microsoft's own communications. I led the production that got it out the door.

Brief

Microsoft and Koto had the strategy and the big idea: light as the metaphor for AI. They needed a partner to turn that idea into a CG world, and to scale it into everything a global launch needs.

Role

I'd built and held BUCK's relationship with the Microsoft lead behind the launch's go-to-market and creative, so BUCK was in the conversation as soon as the search for a partner began. As executive producer, I worked with Microsoft to define the shape and size of our role, so BUCK delivered the most value where it mattered most. I ran a team of 20-plus at BUCK at peak, brought in Ditroit as our CG partner and managed that partnership, and worked side by side with Koto, the Windows and Surface teams, and our live-action partners.

Challenge

We joined a team that was already in motion, and the job was to fit in fast and make the work better without slowing it down. The hardest production problem was retail: assets had to go out the door early, before the rest of the system was fully set, and that took the most complex production planning of the whole project.

What we made

Over about five months, with the first launch in about two: seven films; a New York photo and video shoot whose portraits became hero imagery for the brand; naming; retail; materials for the website; and a library of internal and external communications assets. Ditroit counted more than 100 assets and over 1,000 rendered exports.

Assets

What I protected

The CG lighting was the hardest thing to hold onto. It's difficult for other agencies to reproduce, and easy to lose as a launch scales. I fought for it to stay the hero visual, and found ways to deliver it within the budget.

Outcome

Microsoft was thrilled, and we've since partnered with them on a number of marketing campaigns. It was a landmark launch for the category and the brand, and two years on, the work is still in use. For me, that's the real test: work that lasts.

Concept and brand strategy: Koto. CG: BUCK with Ditroit. Client: Microsoft Windows and Surface.
